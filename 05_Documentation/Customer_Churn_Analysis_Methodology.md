# Customer Churn & Retention Analysis — Methodology

## 1. Python Data Preparation
The raw Excel workbook was processed through data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, churn analysis and analytical dataset preparation.

## 2. Data Validation
Validation covered dimensions, missing values, duplicates, key uniqueness, data types, dates, referential integrity, business rules and Data Dictionary consistency.

## 3. SQL Analysis
Prepared data was loaded into MySQL for database design, data loading, exploratory analysis, churn analysis, management questions, business questions and interview-oriented queries.

## 4. Power BI Modeling
Power BI integrates customer, subscription, payment, customer-service, support-ticket and date information. Date and Churn Date tables support time-based analysis while relationships were validated to avoid ambiguous filter paths.

## 5. DAX
Key measures cover customers, churn, subscriptions, payments, failed payments, support tickets, satisfaction, resolution time, new customers and churn by churn date.

## 6. Dashboard
Three pages were developed:
1. Executive Overview
2. Churn & Retention
3. Customer & Service Insights

## 7. Dashboard Validation
The dashboard was validated for KPI calculations, DAX measures, visual fields, date and ticket-group sorting, percentage formatting, slicers, cross-filtering and readability.

## 8. Interpretation
Findings are treated as associations rather than proof of causation. The dashboard is primarily descriptive and diagnostic.

## 9. Future Analytical Improvement
A customer-level multi-factor churn-risk framework could combine profile, subscription, payment, support, tenure and engagement variables.
